# Stockpile Mod - 6-Tier Progression System

## Overview
The Stockpile mod features a balanced 6-tier progression system with **72 total tools** (9 tools per tier × 8 material sets).

---

## Tier Breakdown

### **Tier 1: Bone**
- **Level:** Wood equivalent
- **Durability:** 80 (between wood 59 and stone 131)
- **Speed:** 2.5F (slightly faster than wood 2.0F)
- **Damage:** 1.5F (HIGHER than wood 0.0F - great for early combat!)
- **Enchantability:** 12
- **Material:** **Bone Meal** (vanilla item - craft 1 bone → 3 bone meal!)
- **Philosophy:** Very accessible early-game tool. High damage for Tier 1 but terrible durability - breaks quickly! Since you get 3 bone meal per bone, repairs are incredibly cheap!

**Tools:** Sword, Spear, Axe, Pickaxe, Shovel, Hoe, Shears, Fishing Rod, Paxel

---

### **Tier 2: Hardened Clay**
- **Level:** Stone equivalent
- **Durability:** 145 (slightly better than stone)
- **Speed:** 4.2F
- **Damage:** 1.0F
- **Enchantability:** 8
- **Material:** Clay balls (baked into hardened clay shards)
- **Philosophy:** Solid stone-level progression with good durability

**Tools:** Sword, Spear, Axe, Pickaxe, Shovel, Hoe, Shears, Fishing Rod, Paxel

---

### **Tier 2: Crystal Glass**
- **Level:** Stone equivalent (HIGH RISK/REWARD)

- **Damage:** 2.0F
- **Enchantability:** 22 (excellent - like gold)
- **Material:** Glass shards
- **Philosophy:** High-speed mining and combat, but tools break quickly. Rewards careful play.

**Tools:** Sword, Spear, Axe, Pickaxe, Shovel, Hoe, Shears, Fishing Rod, Paxel

## Tool Types (9 per tier)

1. **Sword** - Combat weapon
2. **Spear** - Faster attacking weapon with reach
3. **Axe** - Wood chopping & combat
4. **Pickaxe** - Mining stone & ores
5. **Shovel** - Digging dirt, sand, gravel
6. **Hoe** - Farming
7. **Shears** - Shearing sheep, collecting leaves
8. **Fishing Rod** - Fishing
9. **Paxel** - 3-in-1 tool (Pickaxe + Axe + Shovel)

---

## Progression Path

**Early Game:**
1. Bone (Tier 1) - Kill skeletons for bones, craft to bone meal (3 per bone), high damage but breaks fast
2. Hardened Clay (Tier 2) - Gather clay balls, solid stone-level tools
3. Crystal Glass (Tier 2) - High risk/reward option for experienced players

**Mid Game:**
4. Copper (Tier 3) - Balanced progression
5. Obsidian Alloy (Tier 3) - Durable alternative

**Late Game:**
6. Adamantine (Tier 4) - Diamond-level power
7. Voidsteel (Tier 5) - Balanced netherite-level
8. Starlight Steel (Tier 5) - Speed-focused alternative

**End Game:**
9. Dragonsteel (Tier 6) - Ultimate tools after defeating the Ender Dragon

## Compatibility

- **Item Tags:** All paxels tagged under `stockpile:paxels` for cross-mod compatibility
- **Block Mining:** Paxels can mine blocks tagged as pickaxe, axe, OR shovel mineable
- **Mystical Agriculture:** Recipe pattern matches their paxel system
